# Internwise — 2-Week Social Media Comeback Campaign
### Campaign: "Back & Better" | April 7–18, 2026

> **Context for agents:** Internwise has been inactive on social media for approximately one year. This is a carefully paced re-entry campaign — not a volume dump. Every post must re-establish trust, warmth, and value before pushing platform or product. Read `brand_tone.md` before generating any content.

---

## Campaign strategy

### Why this approach
Returning after a long silence requires a different strategy than ongoing content. Audiences need to be reminded why they followed in the first place. The priority order is:

1. **Week 1 — Re-establish the brand** — warm, human, value-first content. No selling. Rebuild the emotional connection.
2. **Week 2 — Build momentum** — increase depth, introduce employer-facing content, begin soft calls-to-action toward internwise.com.

### Platform strategy
We are returning to **6 platforms** simultaneously. Each has a distinct role:

| Platform | Role | Audience focus | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Visual inspiration, tips, student community | Candidates / students | 1x/day |
| **LinkedIn** | Thought leadership, career insight, employer content | Employers + ambitious grads | 1x/day |
| **X (Twitter)** | Sharp takes, stats, conversation sparkers | Candidates + recruiters | 1–2x/day |
| **Facebook** | Community, shareable long-form, groups | Mixed / older graduates | 1x/day |
| **YouTube** | Long-form educational content, shorts | Candidates | 2 videos over 2 weeks |
| **TikTok** | Hook-driven, fast career tips, relatability | Gen Z students | 1x/day |

### Platform addition recommendation
Add **Threads** (Meta's text platform) — it mirrors X but skews toward career and professional content communities. Low effort to repurpose from Instagram/X copy. Recommended to test with 3–4 posts in Week 2.

---

## Content category rotation

Posts rotate across these 5 pillars drawn from the 12 source categories. This prevents repetition and ensures both audiences (candidates and employers) feel served.

| Pillar | Source categories used | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| A — Career inspiration | Mindset & Motivation, Wellbeing & Life Lessons, Personal Development | Candidates |
| B — Practical career advice | CV & Application, Interview Preparation, Communication Skills | Candidates |
| C — Internship world | Internship & Graduate, Career Strategy, Networking & LinkedIn | Candidates |
| D — Future of work | AI & Future of Work, Career Strategy | Both |
| E — Employer & leadership | B2B / Employer Insights, Leadership & Management | Employers |

**Rotation rule for agents:** No two consecutive posts on the same platform should share the same pillar. Alternate between candidate-facing and employer-facing content every 2–3 days.
