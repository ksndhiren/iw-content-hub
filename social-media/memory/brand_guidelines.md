# Internwise — Brand Guidelines

## Who We Are
Internwise is the UK and Europe's internship recruitment specialist, connecting global employers with ambitious students and recent graduates since 2010. We operate across the UK, Europe, Africa, Asia Pacific, and Central/South America. We are a niche platform built specifically for internships, work placements, summer programmes, and entry-level roles.

## Mission & Vision
- **Mission:** Build a portfolio of platforms where job seekers connect directly with employers — removing friction, saving time, and opening doors.
- **Vision:** Reduce youth unemployment rates globally by making internship recruitment accessible, efficient, and human.

## Brand Personality
Warm and human above everything else. We speak like a trusted career mentor.

We are:
- **Encouraging** — we believe in the potential of every candidate
- **Approachable** — no corporate jargon, no gatekeeping
- **Knowledgeable** — we understand the internship market deeply
- **Inclusive** — we celebrate talent from all backgrounds
- **Purposeful** — everything connects back to reducing youth unemployment

We are NOT:
- Salesy or pushy
- Cold or transactional
- Overly formal or stiff
- Generic ("we're hiring!" energy)
- Condescending toward young job seekers

## Voice by Audience

### Candidates (students & graduates)
- Tone: Warm, motivating, peer-like
- Use "you" directly and often
- Lead with encouragement before information
- Celebrate small wins
- Everyday language — no HR jargon
- On-brand: "Your next opportunity is closer than you think." / "Every expert was once a beginner."
- Off-brand: "Leverage your skill set to secure optimal career outcomes." ✗

### Employers & Recruiters
- Tone: Professional but friendly, outcome-focused
- Lead with value and results
- Keep it concise — employers are busy
- Emphasise quality over volume
- On-brand: "Find your next great hire before they graduate." / "Internwise does the searching. You do the hiring."
- Off-brand: "Optimise your talent acquisition pipeline with our platform." ✗

## Content Pillars
1. **Career inspiration** — first-job anxiety, career pivots, success stories, mindset
2. **Internship & job market insights** — most in-demand roles, salary trends, hiring seasons
3. **Practical career advice** — CV writing, interview prep, LinkedIn profiles
4. **Employer spotlight** — celebrating companies hiring on Internwise
5. **Platform & community** — new features, testimonials, milestones, stats

## Tone by Platform

### LinkedIn
- Most formal — but still warm and human
- Longer-form copy OK (up to 1,200 chars for engagement)
- Lead with insight, question, or bold statement
- No slang; conversational but polished
- Audience: employers, recruiters, ambitious graduates

### Instagram
- Visual-first — copy supports the image
- Short punchy captions (under 150 chars for hook)
- Emojis welcome — 2–4 per post, relevant only
- Motivational, aspirational, community-driven
- Audience skews younger — students, early-career

### X (Twitter)
- Concise, sharp, smart — under 240 characters ideally
- One idea per post, no padding
- Questions, hot takes, surprising stats
- Conversational — we reply, we engage

### TikTok
- Highest energy — fast hook in first 2 seconds
- Relatable, real, slightly informal
- Educational content performs well
- Speak-to-camera energy — write as if talking to a friend
- Light humour where natural — never forced
- Audience: students, Gen Z job seekers

## Visual Identity

### Colours
**Primary palette (first choice):**
- Deep blue: `#264D7E`
- Teal: `#5B9291`
- Medium blue: `#3E77B1`
- Light blue: `#5FA7E5`

**Secondary palette (use only when necessary or fits):**
- Warm amber: `#FFB120`
- Soft orange: `#FF9961`
- Dark charcoal: `#333333`
- Light silver: `#E4E8F1`

**Functional:**
- Text on dark: White `#FFFFFF`
- Text on light: Dark charcoal `#333333` or near-black
- Background: Light silver `#E4E8F1` or white `#FFFFFF`

### Typography
- Headings: Inter (Bold or SemiBold)
- Body/captions: DM Sans (Regular or Medium)
- Never more than two fonts in a single asset

### Logo Usage
- Always include the IW icon alongside the wordmark
- Minimum clear space: 40px on all sides
- Do not stretch, compress, recolour, or retype the logo
- Logo: top-left or centred — never bottom-right buried in clutter
- Approved backgrounds: white, navy, coral only
- Logo assets located in: `branding/PNG/`, `branding/SVG/`, `branding/Social Icons/`

### Imagery Style
- Real-world feeling — young professionals in genuine work settings
- Diverse, global talent — reflect our international platform
- Avoid generic stock photo energy (stiff poses, fake smiles)
- Prefer: candid working moments, laptop setups, collaborative scenes, city backdrops (London prominent)
- Bright, clean, uncluttered compositions

### Graphic Design — Mandatory Human Image Rules
- **Every slide in every carousel MUST include a real human photo. Text-only or SVG-only slides are not permitted.**
- Preferred placement: right-side arch + cutout pattern — colored arch shape (`border-radius: 240px 240px 0 0`) on right 45% of canvas, rembg cutout person overlaid
- Hook slides (slide 1): large full-height person cutout on right half of canvas
- Content/topic slides: arch + cutout in right 42% of canvas; all text confined to left 52% (set `right: 530px` on content column)
- CTA/conclusion slides: person on right, content on left
- Stat cards (single posts): arch + cutout on one side, stats/text on the other
- Photos sourced via `pexels_utils.get_cutout()` — always append "isolated plain white background portrait studio" to queries for clean rembg results
- Minimum 2–3 unique Pexels photos per carousel post (may rotate across slides); never reuse the exact same person on adjacent slides
- SVG illustrations are decorative supplements only — never the sole visual element on a slide
- **Content layout rule:** Use CSS `display:flex; flex-direction:column; gap` for the text column — never use stacked `position:absolute; top:Npx` for individual paragraphs within a column. Flex layout prevents text overflow and element overlap when copy wraps unexpectedly.

## Hashtag Strategy

### Core brand (always available)
`#Internwise` `#FindYourInternship` `#InternshipLife`

### Candidate-facing
`#Internship` `#GraduateJobs` `#StudentJobs` `#EntryLevel` `#CareerStart` `#InternshipOpportunity` `#JobSearch` `#GradLife` `#CareerAdvice` `#FirstJob`

### Employer-facing
`#HireInterns` `#GraduateRecruitment` `#TalentAcquisition` `#EarlyCareerHiring` `#InternshipProgram`

### Trending/contextual (search before use)
`#RemoteInternship` `#LondonJobs` `#UKGraduates` `#SummerInternship` `#WorkExperience`

### Rules per platform
- LinkedIn: 3–5 hashtags, placed at end of post
- Instagram: 5–10 hashtags, in caption or first comment
- X: 1–2 hashtags max, woven naturally into copy
- TikTok: 3–6 hashtags in caption, mix of niche and broad

### Formatting rule
Hashtags must always be written in **all lowercase**. Never use CamelCase or PascalCase.
- Correct: `#graduatejobs` `#careeradvice` `#salarytransparency`
- Incorrect: `#GraduateJobs` `#CareerAdvice` `#SalaryTransparency`

## Content Rules
- Never use em dashes (—) in any copy. Use commas, colons, full stops, or restructure the sentence instead.
- Never mention specific company or brand names in social media copy or graphic text. Use generic descriptors instead. Examples: "leading supermarket scheme" not "Aldi", "top accounting firms" not "Big 4" or "Deloitte/PwC/EY/KPMG", "top City law firms" not "Magic Circle", "intelligence services" not "MI5/GCHQ", "large tech firms" not "Google/Amazon/Meta". This applies to both written captions and text rendered inside graphics.

## What to Always Avoid
- Making up statistics, only cite verified data
- Mentioning competitors by name (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, etc.)
- Political commentary
- Overpromising ("get hired in 24 hours", "guaranteed interviews")
- Stereotyping by nationality, background, age, or field
- Condescending language toward young people
- Excessive exclamation marks (one per post max)
- Clichés: "passionate", "rockstar", "ninja", "guru", "hustle culture"
