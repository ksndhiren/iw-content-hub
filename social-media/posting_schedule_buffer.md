### X (Twitter) — 5-Tweet Thread

**Tweet 1 (Slide 1):**
```
Every interviewer has heard "I'm a team player." But very few prove it. The STAR method changes that. Here's how. #InterviewTips #JobSearch
```

**Tweet 2 (Slide 2):**
```
S - Situation: Set the scene. What was the context? Example: "In my final year, our group project lost two members a week before the deadline."
```

**Tweet 3 (Slide 3):**
```
T - Task: What was your specific role? "I stepped up to lead the team and redistribute the workload."
```

**Tweet 4 (Slide 4):**
```
A - Action: What did you actually do? "I created a daily standup schedule, reassigned tasks by strength, handled the research myself."
```

**Tweet 5:**
```
R - Result: What happened? Use numbers. "We delivered on time and scored 78%, the highest in our cohort." Practice STAR with every question. #Internwise #CareerAdvice
```

Nobody teaches you how to network. Not at school, not at university, and definitely not in a job advert.
