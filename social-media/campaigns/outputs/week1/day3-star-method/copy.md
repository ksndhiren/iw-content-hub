## Pillar: C — Practical career advice
## Topic: The STAR Method for Interview Answers

---

### Slide 1 (Hook)
**The STAR Method**
The framework that turns rambling answers into interview gold.

---

### Slide 2
**S — Situation**
Set the scene. What was the context?
"In my final year, our group project lost two members a week before the deadline."

---

### Slide 3
**T — Task**
What was your specific role or responsibility?
"I stepped up to lead the remaining team and redistribute the workload."

---

### Slide 4
**A — Action**
What did you actually do? Be specific.
"I created a daily standup schedule, reassigned tasks by strength, and handled the research section myself."

---

### Slide 5
**R — Result**
What was the outcome? Use numbers if possible.
"We delivered on time and scored 78%, the highest in our cohort."

---

### Caption
Every interviewer has heard "I'm a team player." But very few candidates actually prove it.

The STAR method gives your answers structure, clarity, and impact. Practice it with every behavioural question and watch your confidence grow.

Save this for your next interview prep session.
