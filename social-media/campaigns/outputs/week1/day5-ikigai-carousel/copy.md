### Caption
There is a Japanese concept called Ikigai. It means "a reason for being."

It sits at the intersection of four things: what you love, what the world needs, what you can be paid for, and what you are good at.

Most people optimise for one. The best careers happen when you find the overlap.

This weekend, grab a pen and draw the four circles. Fill them in honestly. You might be surprised how close you already are to finding your thing.

Save this for when you need a reminder.
