### Headline
**5 CV mistakes that get you rejected (before anyone reads your skills)**

---

### Tips
1. **No measurable results** — "Helped with social media" tells them nothing. Add numbers.
2. **Generic objective statement** — "Seeking a challenging role..." belongs in 2010. Replace with a sharp personal summary.
3. **Walls of text** — Recruiters scan for 6 seconds. Use bullet points and white space.
4. **Irrelevant experience** — Your paper round at 14 is not helping. Tailor every application.
5. **Typos and formatting errors** — One spelling mistake can end your chances. Proofread twice.
