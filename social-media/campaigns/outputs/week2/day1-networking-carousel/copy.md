### Slide 1 (Hook)
**Nobody teaches you how to network.**
So here is how.

---

### Slide 2
**1. Start with who you already know.**
Your lecturers, classmates, family friends. You do not need 500 connections. You need 5 real ones.

---

### Slide 3
**2. Give before you ask.**
Share their post. Comment something thoughtful. Congratulate them on a win. Build the relationship before you need a favour.

---

### Slide 4
**3. Send the message anyway.**
"Hi, I admire your work in [field]. I am a student exploring this area. Would you have 15 minutes for a quick chat?" Most people say yes.

---

### Slide 5 (CTA)
**The best time to network is before you need a job.**

### Caption
Nobody teaches you how to network. Not at school, not at university, and definitely not in a job advert.

But networking is not schmoozing at events or sending "Hi, I noticed we have a mutual connection" messages to strangers.

It is simpler than that. Start with people you already know. Give before you ask. And when you do reach out to someone new, be genuine, be brief, and be specific.

The best opportunities often come from relationships you built before you needed them.
