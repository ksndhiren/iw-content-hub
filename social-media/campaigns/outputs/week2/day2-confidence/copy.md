The voice in your head is not always telling the truth.

It tells you that everyone else has it figured out. That one more rejection means you are not good enough. That confidence is something other people are born with.

None of that is true.

Confidence is built through action. Through showing up even when you are not ready. Through applying again after the no. Through comparing your journey to where you were six months ago, not to someone else's highlight reel.

The people you look up to most, they were once exactly where you are now.
