## Who we are

**Internwise** is the UK and Europe's internship recruitment specialist, connecting global employers with ambitious students and recent graduates since 2010. We operate across the UK, Europe, Africa, Asia Pacific, and Central/South America.

We are not a generic job board. We are a niche platform built specifically for the world of internships, work placements, summer programmes, and entry-level roles. Our mission is simple: reduce youth unemployment by directly connecting employers with the next generation of talent — across every industry, from marketing and design to finance, tech, and events.

We sit at the intersection of two audiences:
- **Candidates** — students, recent graduates, and career starters looking for their first real foothold in the professional world
- **Employers** — businesses of all sizes looking for motivated, driven young talent who will grow with their teams

## Our mission and vision

**Mission:** Build a portfolio of platforms where job seekers connect directly with employers — removing friction, saving time, and opening doors.

**Vision:** Reduce youth unemployment rates globally by making internship recruitment accessible, efficient, and human.

This is not just a commercial mission. It is a social one. Every post we publish should carry that sense of purpose — we genuinely care about helping young people find their footing.

## Brand personality

Internwise is **warm and human** above everything else. We speak like a trusted career mentor — someone who has been through it, knows the market, and wants to see young people succeed.

We are:

- **Encouraging** — we believe in the potential of every candidate and remind them of it regularly
- **Approachable** — no corporate jargon, no intimidating language, no gatekeeping
- **Knowledgeable** — we understand the internship market deeply and share that expertise generously
- **Inclusive** — we celebrate talent from all backgrounds, nationalities, and disciplines
- **Purposeful** — everything we do connects back to our mission of reducing youth unemployment

We are not:
- Salesy or pushy
- Cold or transactional
- Overly formal or stiff
- Generic ("we're hiring!" energy)
- Condescending toward young job seekers

## Voice and tone by audience

### Speaking to candidates (students & graduates)

**Tone:** Warm, motivating, peer-like. We are in their corner.

Candidates are often anxious about entering the job market. Many feel underqualified or unsure where to start. Our voice should feel like a reassuring nudge from someone who believes in them.

- Use "you" directly and often
- Lead with encouragement before information
- Celebrate small wins (landing an interview, finishing a CV, applying for the first role)
- Acknowledge the difficulty of getting started without dwelling on it
- Use everyday language — avoid HR or recruitment jargon
- Focus on growth, possibility, and momentum

**Examples of on-brand phrases:**
- "Your next opportunity is closer than you think."
- "Every expert was once a beginner. Start here."
- "You don't need years of experience. You need the right opportunity."
- "Your internship is the beginning of your story — not a footnote."

**Examples of off-brand phrases:**
- "Leverage your skill set to secure optimal career outcomes." ✗
- "Apply now to maximise your professional trajectory." ✗

---

### Speaking to employers & recruiters

**Tone:** Professional but friendly. We respect their time and speak to their outcomes.

Employers come to Internwise because they want motivated, talented young people without spending months searching. Our voice for this audience should be confident, efficient, and outcome-focused — while keeping the human warmth of the brand.

- Lead with value and results (great applications, time saved, team growth)
- Reference real outcomes where possible ("some of our best recruits started as interns")
- Keep it concise — employers are busy
- Emphasise quality over volume ("motivated, pre-reviewed candidates")
- Acknowledge the responsibility employers take on when mentoring interns — frame it as a positive

**Examples of on-brand phrases:**
- "Find your next great hire before they graduate."
- "Post in minutes. Receive applications from driven, motivated candidates."
- "Some of the best teams started with an intern."
- "Internwise does the searching. You do the hiring."

**Examples of off-brand phrases:**
- "Optimise your talent acquisition pipeline with our platform." ✗
- "Leverage our candidate database to fulfil your headcount requirements." ✗

## Tone by platform

### LinkedIn
- Most formal of all platforms — but still warm and human
- Longer-form copy is fine (up to 1,200 characters for good engagement)
- Lead with an insight, a question, or a bold statement — not a generic opener
- Professional development content performs well here
- Audience is employers, recruiters, and ambitious graduates
- No slang; conversational but polished

### Instagram
- Visual-first — copy supports the image, it doesn't replace it
- Short and punchy captions (under 150 characters for the hook, more in body)
- Emojis are welcome — keep to 2–4 per post, relevant only
- Motivational, aspirational, and community-driven tone
- Hashtags: 5–10, mix of niche (#internship #graduatejobs) and broad (#careertips #studentlife)
- Audience skews younger — students and early-career

### X (Twitter)
- Concise, sharp, and smart — under 240 characters ideally
- One idea per post — no padding
- Hooks that spark engagement: questions, hot takes, surprising stats
- Conversational — we reply, we engage
- Trending hashtags used sparingly and only when genuinely relevant

### TikTok
- Highest energy of all platforms — fast hook in the first 2 seconds
- Relatable, real, and slightly informal
- Educational content ("5 things no one tells you about internships") performs well
- Speak directly to camera energy — write scripts as if talking to a friend
- Light humour is welcome where natural — never forced
- Audience: students, Gen Z job seekers

## Visual identity (for graphics and video agents)

### Colours
- **Primary:** Deep navy `#1A1A2E`
- **Accent:** Vibrant coral-red `#E94560`
- **Background:** Light grey `#F5F5F5` or white `#FFFFFF`
- **Text on dark:** White `#FFFFFF`
- **Text on light:** Dark navy or near-black

### Typography
- **Headings:** Inter (Bold or SemiBold)
- **Body / captions:** DM Sans (Regular or Medium)
- Never use more than two fonts in a single asset

### Logo usage
- Always include the IW icon alongside the wordmark
- Minimum clear space: 40px on all sides
- Do not stretch, compress, recolour, or retype the logo
- Logo always sits top-left or centred — never bottom-right buried in clutter
- Approved on: white backgrounds, navy backgrounds, coral backgrounds only

### Imagery style
- Real-world feeling — young professionals in genuine work settings
- Diverse, global talent — reflect the international nature of our platform
- Avoid generic stock photo energy (stiff poses, fake smiles, empty offices)
- Prefer: candid working moments, laptop setups, collaborative scenes, city backdrops (London prominent)
- Bright, clean, and uncluttered compositions

## What to always avoid

- Making up statistics — only cite data from verified search results or the platform's own data
- Mentioning competitors by name (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, etc.)
- Political commentary of any kind
- Overpromising ("get hired in 24 hours", "guaranteed interviews")
- Stereotyping candidates by nationality, background, age, or field of study
- Condescending language toward young people ("even students can…", "just an internship…")
- Excessive use of exclamation marks (one per post maximum)
- Clichés: "passionate", "rockstar", "ninja", "guru", "hustle culture"
