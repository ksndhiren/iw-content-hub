# Infographic lane: November 2026 topics and illustration prompts

21 weekday slots (Mon 2 Nov to Mon 30 Nov), plus 2 spares = **23 images**.
Revision 3, 30 Oct 2026. Built from internwise.co.uk Search Console data
(90 days), YouTube Data API views, SerpApi UK Google results, external research,
and a check against what the education lane has already covered.

---

## How these were chosen

Every topic has to pass at least one evidence test, and it has to be a
*save* or *send* format: reference material someone comes back to or
forwards to a friend. Instagram ranks posts mainly on DM shares and saves.
LinkedIn ranks them on dwell time and saves.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SEARCH** | Google demand reaching internwise.co.uk, 30 Jul to 27 Oct 2026 (figures are impressions) |
| **TIMELY** | Tied to something happening in November 2026 |
| **DATA** | Built around a fresh, citable UK stat (sources at the bottom) |
| **EMPLOYER** | For hiring managers. Our best LinkedIn posts were employer and founder pieces |
| **YOUTUBE** | Views on on-topic YouTube videos (UK region, published since Oct 2024), YouTube Data API |
| **SERP** | UK Google results via SerpApi: the questions in the "People also ask" box (PAA) and related searches |

**What the site data says.** Over 90 days the site had 270k search impressions
and 13.5k clicks. The biggest content signal by far is **career explainers**,
meaning "what is X / what does X do / how do I get into X":

| Page | Impressions | Position |
|---|---|---|
| Types of marketing roles | 33,836 | 10.4 |
| What is asset management | 10,520 | 23.8 |
| How to get into journalism | 5,069 | 10.2 |
| How to become an analyst | 4,128 | 8.9 |
| What is a sales executive | 3,068 | 8.0 (plus about 19k impressions on "sales executive meaning" queries) |

The first list had none of these. They now run as a **Monday "Career Map" series**.

**Weekly rhythm**

| Day | Slot |
|---|---|
| Monday | Career Map |
| Tuesday | Application craft |
| Wednesday | Data and timely topics |
| Thursday | Employer |
| Friday | Practical reference |

Each card's caption should link to the matching site page where one exists.
Those pages rank on page 1–2 but convert badly; marketing roles turns 33.8k
impressions into 46 clicks. Social traffic to them is a direct win.

## Technical spec (applies to every image)

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | **1024 × 1024** |
| Background | **Transparent PNG** (the card places it on deep navy `#0E2141`) |
| Text in image | **None.** No letters, numbers, labels or signage anywhere |
| Margin | Keep the subject within the centre 85%, because the card crops the edges |
| Naming | `infographic_01_marketing_roles.png` and so on, as in the table below |

Generate all 23 in **one session** so lighting, line weight and proportions stay
consistent. If your tool supports a style reference or fixed seed, lock it after
image 1 and reuse it.

## Style block (paste this ahead of every subject line)

> Flat vector illustration, modern editorial style, clean rounded shapes, soft
> subtle gradients, confident medium line weight, gentle top-left light source.
> Colour palette strictly: deep navy #0E2141, amber #FFB120, coral #FF6B6B,
> mint #7FDBB6, sky blue #5FA7E5, soft purple #7B5CE6, cream #FAF5EC.
> One friendly UK university student or recent graduate, aged 19–25, waist-up or
> seated, three-quarter view, warm and capable expression, never stressed,
> cartoonish or childish. Props are simple and geometric. Transparent background.
> Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos or brand marks anywhere in the image.
> Vary the character's appearance, hair and skin tone image to image; keep the
> drawing style, proportions and palette identical across the whole set.

For the four **EMPLOYER** topics (04, 09, 14, 19), swap the character line for:
> One friendly UK small-business founder or hiring manager, aged 28–45, waist-up,
> three-quarter view, approachable and capable expression.
