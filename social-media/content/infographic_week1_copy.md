**LinkedIn**
> One of the most-searched questions we see: does an internship count as work experience?

**Alt text:** Infographic titled "Does an Internship Count as Experience?". Paid internship, unpaid internship, placement year and volunteering count, and work shadowing partly counts. How to write it, mistakes to avoid, and a four-point check for a strong CV entry.
