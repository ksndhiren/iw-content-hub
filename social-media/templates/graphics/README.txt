Brand-locked image templates directory.

Place template files here organized by type:
- stat_card/       — key metric or job market stat templates
- job_spotlight/   — featured job listing templates
- employer_spotlight/ — hiring company feature templates
- tip_card/        — career advice visual list templates
- carousel/        — multi-slide format templates

Templates should use brand colors:
- Primary: #1A1A2E (deep navy)
- Accent: #E94560 (vibrant coral-red)
- Background: #F5F5F5 (light gray) or white
- Fonts: Inter (headings), DM Sans (body)
