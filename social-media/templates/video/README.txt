Video template assets directory.

Place the following assets here:
- intro/       — brand intro animations (3-5 seconds)
- outro/       — brand outro with CTA (3-5 seconds)
- lower_third/ — name/title lower-third overlays
- music/       — approved background music tracks
- transitions/ — brand-consistent transition effects

All video assets should follow brand guidelines:
- Primary: #1A1A2E (deep navy)
- Accent: #E94560 (vibrant coral-red)
- Font: Inter (on-screen text)
