## Spoken Script
Not every internship is worth accepting. Before you accept an internship, check whether
it will actually move you forward. First: The title matters less than the evidence you
can build. Second: Good internships include feedback, ownership, and useful exposure.
Third: If the role is vague, ask what you will actually do each week. The rule is
simple: A useful internship gives real tasks, feedback, a named manager, and evidence
you can show later. Save this before saying yes to an internship.
