## Spoken Script
A rejection is data. It is not your identity. Turn rejection into a better next
application without spiralling. First: Log where you dropped out: CV, test, interview,
final round. Second: Change one controllable thing before the next attempt. Third: Keep
a rejection-safe pipeline so one no does not stop everything. The rule is simple:
Separate signal from story: log what happened, adjust one thing, keep moving. Keep
going. Search your next opportunity at internwise.co.uk
