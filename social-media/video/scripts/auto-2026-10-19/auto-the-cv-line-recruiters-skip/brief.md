## Spoken Script
Your CV is not being read the way you think it is. I will show you the part of your CV
that quietly loses interviews. First: Recruiters scan for role fit before they read
every line. Second: A task without a result feels like a job description, not evidence.
Third: Your strongest proof should appear above the fold, not hidden on page two. The
rule is simple: Replace vague responsibility lines with proof: action, skill, result.
Save this before you edit your CV tonight.
